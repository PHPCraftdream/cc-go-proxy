## Decisions
- Chose to keep `busy` background requests on HTTP over trying to serve them on the websocket: they cannot be a delta and
  would replace the agent's chain.
- Chose a 1-minute burst window over requiring a close frame for age lowering: the backend's own 30-minute cut arrives
  without a close frame too (seen 15:47:18), so close frames cannot tell the two apart.
- Chose to tolerate only added keys with value `false` in tool-call arguments, rejecting a general "semantically equal"
  rule: true or other added values still break the chain.
- Chose a 128-byte prefix key for side messages over a whole-message hash; a false match only sends one request over HTTP.
- Fixed CI-found flakes (installer rate limit, proxydial deadline race) at their cause in the same PR, per the user's rule.
