## Decisions
- chose `MIT OR Apache-2.0` over MIT alone: every dependency is permissive, and parts derived from upstream stay available under MIT.
- chose "one login per provider, profile name = provider name" for several Codex accounts (`ccgp codex2`) over adding an account field to profiles: it matches how credentials are stored and needs no new concept.
- chose to strip thinking blocks whenever source and destination providers differ (claude counts as one) over keeping them for imports: signatures belong to the provider/account that wrote them.
- chose editing `config.ktav` in place (text-level, validated, backed up) over a separate profiles.d/ directory: one source of truth, no loader changes.
- rejected any pass-through of a Claude subscription (Anthropic's terms restrict routing its credentials); only other backends and API keys are used.
